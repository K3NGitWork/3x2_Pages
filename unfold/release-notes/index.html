Release Notes

What changed in each version of Unfold. The Mac and iPhone & iPad versions have their own notes.

1.0.1Latest

25 October 2026

Download

New

  • Settings > General and the welcome screen show whether Unfold is in Finder's right-click menu, with Turn On… to switch it on.
  • With many archives, the one being unfolded is on top of the list and finished ones move to the bottom.

Fixed

  • A password you just typed is tried first for the other archives waiting for one, so Unfold doesn't ask again.

1.0

23 October 2026

iOS App Store

The first version for iPhone and iPad.

New

  • Unfold right in Files: Share › Unfold Archive. The app stays closed, and the files appear next to the archive.
  • Open archives from Files, Mail, Safari or any app with “Open in Unfold”.
  • Multipart RAR sets unfold as one.
  • Saved passwords are tried for you, and stay in your Keychain.
iPhone and iPad get Unfold from the iOS App Store only; it updates through the App Store.
