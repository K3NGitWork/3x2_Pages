Unfold
RAR, ZIP & 7z, right in Finder.

Unfold opens archives the way you expect: one click, the files appear next to the archive, done. Multipart RAR sets (name.part1.rar … part12.rar) unfold as one, whichever part you open, and saved passwords are tried for you.

  • Right-click in Finder: Unfold Here, Unfold to Folder, Unfold to…
  • Double-click, drop on the window, Quick Action, or Shortcuts
  • RAR (incl. RAR5), ZIP (incl. AES), 7z, TAR, GZ, BZ2, XZ, ZST, LZ4 and more
  • Private by design: everything happens on your device, no account, no tracking
  • Free, every feature, no ads, no in-app purchases

For macOS 26, iOS 26 and iPadOS 26.

Get Unfold

On the App Store for Mac, iPhone and iPad, or as a download for Mac. Both are free and have every feature. The download is signed and notarized by Apple. When a new version is out, Unfold offers it when you open it, and installs it with one click.

Download for Mac All versions

Open the DMG and drag Unfold to Applications. For the Finder menu, turn on System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions › Finder › Unfold.
