And the details

Whole folders

Drop a folder and every video inside it joins the queue, subfolders too. Other files are left alone.

Saved next to the original

The new .mkv lands beside the original, or in a folder you choose. Nothing is ever overwritten.

Three presets

Smaller, Balanced or Best in Settings › Quality. Balanced shows no visible loss for most videos.

Fast hardware mode

Uses the Mac’s media engine instead of x265. Much faster, but files come out larger.

Pause any time

Pause and Resume pick up where they left off. Stop keeps the rest of the queue for later.

Your videos stay yours

Everything happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is counted.

Questions

Will my video look worse?

With the Balanced preset, most videos show no visible difference. For footage you want to keep at its best, choose Best in Settings › Quality. The original is never changed or deleted.

Why .mkv?

MKV holds every audio track, subtitle and chapter of the original as it is. It plays in IINA, VLC, Infuse and most TVs and media players. QuickTime Player doesn’t open it.

How much smaller will it get?

Typically 40 to 60%. It depends on the video: phone and camera footage shrinks a lot, videos that are already compressed hard shrink less. If the new file isn’t smaller, Encode deletes it and keeps the original (“Not smaller · kept the original”).

What is fast hardware mode?

It encodes with the Mac’s media engine instead of x265. That is many times faster, but the files come out larger for the same quality. It is off by default.
