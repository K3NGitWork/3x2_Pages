Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Encode doesn’t collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your videos never leave your Mac.

Encode is made by 3x2 Studio (Sebastian Dietrich). This policy covers Encode for Mac.

What Encode works with, and where it stays

  • Videos you add. Encode reads the videos you drop or choose and writes the new files next to them or to a folder you choose. All of it happens on your Mac; nothing is uploaded.
  • Settings. Your preferences (where to save, the preset, fast hardware mode) are stored on your Mac.

None of this is ever sent to 3x2 Studio or anyone else.

Updates

Encode checks for updates once a day by loading a small file from this website, hosted by GitHub, and downloads new versions from GitHub. Like any web request, this shows GitHub your IP address; Encode sends nothing else (no system profile). You can turn the automatic check off in Settings › About › Updates; Check for Updates… in the Encode menu always works.

Tips

Encode is free. This website has a “Buy Me a Coffee” link that opens Ko-fi; Ko-fi’s privacy policy applies there. Encode itself has no such link and doesn’t track anything.

Crash reports

If you agreed to share analytics with app developers in your Mac’s settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports to 3x2 Studio. They contain technical information about the crash and no personal data. You can change this under Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Encode doesn’t collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hello3x2@outlook.de
